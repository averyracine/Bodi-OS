# B2B outreach

Find the right buyers and write outreach that gets replies. Use the **Apollo.io**
MCP tools (`mcp__Apollo_io__*`) for real people/company search and enrichment —
**never fabricate names, emails, or company data.** If Apollo requires auth,
surface that and stop.

## Ideal customer profiles

- **inspiredsurgicalsupplies.com** — B2B buyers of surgical/medical supplies.
  Targets: surgery centers, ambulatory surgical centers, hospitals, private
  practices, group purchasing. Titles: Procurement/Purchasing Manager, Materials
  Manager, OR Manager, Practice Administrator, Supply Chain. Angle: quality,
  reliable fulfillment, better pricing/terms, easy reordering.
- **inspiringphysicians.com** — physicians themselves (and practice leaders).
  Titles: Physician/MD/DO by specialty, Medical Director, Practice Owner. Angle:
  career growth, community, reducing burnout, education — peer-to-peer, low-hype.

## Workflow

1. **Define ICP** — brand, target titles, industries/keywords, company size,
   geography. Infer from context; confirm only what's missing.
2. **Find accounts/contacts** — use Apollo search + match/enrich tools
   (e.g. people/companies search, people match, organization enrich) to build a
   list with name, title, company, and verified contact fields Apollo returns.
3. **Segment** — group by persona/specialty so messaging can be tailored.
4. **Draft sequences** — for each segment, write a 3–4 touch sequence:
   - Touch 1 (email): personalized opener (specific to their role/org), one
     clear value prop, soft CTA. ≤ 120 words, one ask.
   - Touch 2 (email, +3d): new angle / proof point (case, guarantee, spec).
   - Touch 3 (LinkedIn or email, +4d): short nudge + resource.
   - Touch 4 (email, +7d): breakup / last-value message.
5. **Compliance** — B2B, CAN-SPAM: real sender identity, opt-out, no misleading
   subject lines. Healthcare buyers value accuracy — no overclaiming.

## Execution

- Only create/modify anything in Apollo (sequences, contacts, tasks) after the
  user confirms the specific action — these are outward-facing writes.
- Deliver the prospect list as a clean table and the sequences as ready-to-send
  copy with subject lines and send-day offsets.

## Guidance

- Personalize the first line to the person/company, not a mail-merge token.
- One ask per message; make the CTA low-friction (a question, not a demo push).
- Lead with their problem, not your catalog.
- Keep lists tight and well-targeted over large and generic — reply rate > volume.
